You can follow a peer around a space. Follow sits next to Jump and Go to on a peer's row and follows whichever of their sessions moved last; with the sessions toggle on, each session row has a Follow that stays on that session. While you follow, a deck that lays planks side by side shows theirs: the same planks in their order, scrolled to the plank at the front of theirs, with each plank keeping their top line at the top. A flattened deck opens the plank they attend, at the path they opened it from. Neither moves your caret or focus, and they send their location every 100 ms instead of every second. A plank your deck has no URL binding for (a type from a plugin you lack) is left out, and the deck slides to the nearest plank of theirs it holds instead. Scrolling, clicking or typing in a plank, attending an object outside what you show of them, going back or forward in history, or pressing Escape stops it, and so does the leader being gone from the space for five seconds. A follow pauses while you cannot open what they attend and picks up again once you can. The Members tab shows a dot while you follow, and the leader's row sits first in its list with Stop. A `Locator` reveal takes `follow` to scroll without moving the caret, a deck companion's `badge` atom can hold `true` to draw a dot, `AppCapabilities.Layout` gains `flat` and `front`, the plank a sliding deck is scrolled to, which is unset until the deck has measured the planks it shows, and `LayoutOperation.Set` takes `focus: false` to focus no plank. A schema form's boolean field carries its JSON path as `data-testid`, as text fields do. Leading shows too, in indigo on every screen: the Members tab's dot appears on both sides, a follower's leader row rises to the top of Members and carries Stop and a Cursor / Laser switch for your pointer, and a leader sees its followers under "Following you". Every plank heading, companions included, washes in indigo over a one-pixel underline while you follow or are followed, through a new `AppCapabilities.HeadingAccent` the deck applies, and those people's avatars ring in indigo. A follower's deck also opens the companions the leader has open, in the same tab. Everyone in a follow sees each other's pointers over planks and companions, placed on the same word or spot however each window wraps the content (text editors name a pointer's place by document position through a small `@dxos/ui-types` DOM protocol); a laser's tail traces its path, and moving your mouse keeps the follow. `AppCapabilities.Layout` gains `companions`, the items showing their companion and its tab, and a deck companion takes a `badgeHue` for its rail dot.
