The deck shows a plank's companion only when the plank and the companion both fit at their minimum widths. It caps the companion so the pair never runs under the end sidebar, which used to hide the companion's close button. In flat mode, with breadcrumbs, a plank header's controls sit at the header's end. Opening settings closes the companion.
