Sheets gain a Timeline companion that plots a range with one event per row (date, category, title, description) along a vertical spine, with linear or log scale, per-category visibility, and a card showing each event's description on hover. `@dxos/react-ui-plot` exports the `Timeline` component, `TimelineOptions`, and the `toTimelineEvents` and `toTime` helpers.
