Sheets gain a Timeline companion that plots a range with one event per row (date, category, title, description) along a vertical spine. It offers a left, center or right spine, linear, log or even spacing (animated when switched), per-category visibility, optional dates in labels, labels sized to fit, a text filter, click and arrow-key selection, and a card with each event's description. `@dxos/react-ui-plot` exports the `Timeline` component, `TimelineOptions`, and the `toTimelineEvents`, `toTime` and `matchesTimelineFilter` helpers.
