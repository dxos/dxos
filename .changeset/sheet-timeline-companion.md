Sheets gain a Timeline companion that plots a range with one event per row (date, category, title, description) along a vertical spine. Categories can nest by path (`Tech/AI`) and show as toggle tags grouped under their parent, in a chosen order and colors; the timeline offers a left, center or right spine, linear, log or even spacing (animated when switched), optional dates in labels, labels sized to fit, a text filter, click and arrow-key selection, and a card with each event's description. `@dxos/react-ui-plot` exports the `Timeline` component, `TimelineOptions`, and helpers for reading and arranging events.
