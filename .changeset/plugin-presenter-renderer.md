Presentations render slides in a Slidev markdown subset with layouts, click steps, video backgrounds and embedded object surfaces, print the whole deck, one slide per page, and take a theme (`default`, `light`, `dark`, `serif`, with color and font overrides on a Presentation); reveal.js is removed. `react-ui-syntax-highlighter` exports `renderRows`, and `SyntaxHighlighter` takes a `themeMode` prop.
