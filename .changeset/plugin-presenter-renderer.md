Presentations render slides in a Slidev markdown subset with layouts, click steps, video backgrounds and embedded object surfaces, print the whole deck, one slide per page, and take a theme (`default`, `light`, `dark`, `serif`, with color and font overrides on a Presentation). A Presentation's slides open in an editor with a slide toolbar (layout, click steps, columns, objects, background, notes) and a live preview below it or in the companion, kept in step with the cursor both ways; reveal.js is removed. `react-ui-syntax-highlighter` exports `renderRows`, and `SyntaxHighlighter` takes a `themeMode` prop.
