Presenting a Markdown document now follows edits to it, keeping the presenter on the same slide and fragment, and the slides scale to fit when the companion or window is resized. Presenting fullscreen works again: it no longer rewrites the URL or blanks the screen, and it hides the companion tab strip. Narrow decks scale like wide ones instead of switching to Reveal's scroll view.
