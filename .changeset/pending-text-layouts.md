Pending text can now stream in a whole paragraph with blank lines around it or an inline correction that replaces a range, and its confirm/cancel buttons float above the preview once the text settles. The typewriter cursor stays at the end of streamed text, and a menu now closes when a click outside it lands on an element that is redrawn.
