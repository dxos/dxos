The deck companion now opens beside the attended plank instead of at the end of the deck: it shares that plank's container, split by a draggable seam (the same `Splitter` the tiling deck uses), and follows attention as the user moves between planks. The "open companion" control is offered on every plank that has one, and a URL restoring a companion attends the plank it was anchored to.
