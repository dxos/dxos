Adds `Drawer`, a panel that slides in from an edge of the viewport on Ark's drawer machine: swipe to dismiss, snap points for a bottom sheet, a grabber, modal and non-modal variants, a swipe area that opens it from the edge, and a `push` mode in which the panel is part of the page's layout and pushes its neighbours aside as it is dragged.
