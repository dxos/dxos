In the slide editor, slot markers such as `::right::` and click tags without attributes are atomic: the caret steps over them and a delete removes them whole. Two layouts are added: `three-cols` (`::middle::`, `::right::`) and `two-by-two` (`::right::`, `::bottom-left::`, `::bottom-right::`); "Start the next column" inserts the marker after the one at the caret. Typing a `layout:` value in the frontmatter offers the matching layouts with their names, in two columns. The slide toolbar's layout picker is an icon button, and every layout has a translated name in the picker. In fullscreen, the overlay pager stays visible while the pointer is on it. Text fonts include Space Grotesk, Mali, Geist, Orbitron, Unbounded, OpenDyslexic and LXGW WenKai (Chinese), loaded on demand and kept out of the web app's install-time cache. Frontmatter `bleed: left` or `bleed: right` takes a column slide's outer column to the slide's edges, so an embedded object can fill it. An assistant skill, `PresenterSkill` (enabled on Presentations), creates presentations from Slidev markdown slides, adds or links sections, lists the layouts and frontmatter keys, and presents a deck. Code blocks are padded, and a fence's `{lines:true}` option numbers their lines. The deck and slide components are now composites, `Deck.*` and `Slide.*`, with a headless `Root`.
