In a presented deck, left, right, `[` and `]` move between slides past any click steps, up and down step through a slide's click steps, and Cmd or Ctrl with left or right jumps to the first or last slide. Up and down no longer jump to the deck's ends. In a Presentation's thumbnail grid, Space starts presenting from the current slide.
