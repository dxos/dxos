Durable operations are declared in two stages like plain ones: `Operation.makeDurable({ ... })` returns an `Operation.DurableDefinition` and `Operation.withDurableHandler(...)` attaches its handler. `DurableOperation.fromOperation` moves to `Process.fromOperation`, and `@dxos/compute-runtime`'s `ProcessOperationInvoker` (now also exported at the package root, with `make`, `layer` and `Service`) spawns every invocation through `Process.Manager`; its `invokeFiber`/`attachFiber`/`OperationFiber` are removed in favour of `Process.Manager` — spawn a child with `Process.spawn`, read its result with `Process.awaitOutput`, and reattach with the new `Process.Manager.attach` (which also reaches exited processes). Inside a process, `Process.ManagerService` now links spawned processes to it by default, and `InvokeOptions.on === 'edge'` spawns the operation's process on the EDGE runtime hosting `spaceId`. `Trigger.Monitor`/`Trigger.TriggerMonitorService` are renamed `Trigger.Manager`/`Trigger.ManagerService`, and `@dxos/compute-runtime`'s `TriggerMonitor` module is now `TriggerManager`. `Process.Handle` is merged into `Process.Process`: a process from `processTree`/`list` is a cheap snapshot of its `Process.Data` that reaches its runtime only when a live member (`submitInput`, `status`, `rpc`, …) is used. The handle's `parentId` is now `parentPid`, and `Process.Handle.Any` is `Process.Any`.
