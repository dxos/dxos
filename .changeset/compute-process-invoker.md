'@dxos/compute': minor

Durable operations are declared in two stages like plain ones: `Operation.makeDurable({ ... })` returns an `Operation.DurableDefinition` and `Operation.withDurableHandler(...)` attaches its handler. The process-backed operation invoker moves from `@dxos/compute-runtime` (`ProcessOperationInvoker`) to `Operation.makeProcessInvoker`, with its default layer and `fromOperation` in `@dxos/compute/DurableOperation`; `invokeFiber`/`attachFiber`/`OperationFiber` are removed in favour of `Process.Manager` — spawn a child with `DurableOperation.spawn`, read its result with `Process.awaitOutput`, and reattach with the new `Process.Manager.attach` (which also reaches exited processes). Inside a process, `Process.ManagerService` now links spawned processes to it by default, and `InvokeOptions.on === 'edge'` spawns the operation's process on the EDGE runtime hosting `spaceId`. `Trigger.Monitor`/`Trigger.TriggerMonitorService` are renamed `Trigger.Manager`/`Trigger.ManagerService`, and `@dxos/compute-runtime`'s `TriggerMonitor` module is now `TriggerManager`.
