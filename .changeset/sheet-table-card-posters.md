`Card.Poster` takes content other than an image and a `position='end'` that bleeds it to the card's bottom edge; grids gain `useFitWidth` to fit columns to a card's width by their content. Sheets get a card preview of their first chart or their grid, and the table card shows its grid at the same fitted width.
