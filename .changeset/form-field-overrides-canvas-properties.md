Add `fieldOverrides` to `Form.Root` for per-field label, description, placeholder, readonly, hidden and indeterminate (multi-object "Mixed") values, a `fixed` grid in form layouts, and an indeterminate `Switch`; Enter in a text field commits it as leaving it does. The canvas properties panel floats over the scene, edits several selected elements at once, and uses the standard number fields for geometry and font size; canvas shortcuts fire only while the canvas has focus.
