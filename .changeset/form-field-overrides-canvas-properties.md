Add `fieldOverrides` to `Form.Root` for per-field label, description, placeholder, readonly, hidden and indeterminate (multi-object "Mixed") values, a `fixed` grid in form layouts, and an indeterminate `Switch`; Enter in a text field commits it as leaving it does. The canvas properties panel floats over the scene and edits several selected elements at once with the standard form fields; nodes gain a ports-per-side property, a link dropped on empty canvas creates a copy of its source shape, a selected node hides its ports, and canvas shortcuts fire only while the canvas has focus.
