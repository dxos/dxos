The `display` slide font now loads Poiret One instead of naming a face nothing fetched, so a deck set in it showed the fallback sans on every machine that did not happen to have the font installed.

`<Box variant="lockup">` draws a brand mark where a heading would go, at a heading's scale. `<Box variant="fill">` draws its picture past the column's edges, since art whose own drawing stops short of its frame otherwise leaves its lines ending in mid-air inside the column.
