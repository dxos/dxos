The project timeline (Gantt) draws its chart again beside the lane names, and its panel scrolls vertically with a thin scrollbar when the lanes outgrow it. An object card's title is one truncated line by default (pass `lines` to `ObjectCard.Header` to clamp to more), and the card popover is only as tall as its content rather than at least `--spacing-card-min-height`.
