`@dxos/plugin-agent` and `@dxos/plugin-crm` are published, so EDGE can host the agent's operations (fact reading, watches, relays) behind its Discord bot. The agent reads each conversation turn into RDF facts, recalls them across conversations, and keeps one-time and ongoing watches that notify people with updates written from the conversation's context.
