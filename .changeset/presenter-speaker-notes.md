'@dxos/plugin-presenter': minor
---

Presenter shows speaker notes while presenting (subtitles or a separate window, chosen in its settings), gives each slide an optional desired length, and adds extension points for other plugins: a toolbar slot, an overlay slot and a presentation event handler. Studio renders `audio/*` variants and exports its `VariantRenderer` role as `StudioSurface`. Adds the private `plugin-recorder`, which records a presentation's voice-over.
