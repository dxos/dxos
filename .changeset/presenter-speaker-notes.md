Presenter shows speaker notes while presenting (subtitles or a separate window, chosen in its settings), gives each slide an optional desired length, and adds extension points for other plugins: a toolbar slot, an overlay slot and a presentation event handler. Studio renders `audio/*` variants. Adds the private `plugin-recorder`, which records a presentation's voice-over. react-ui adds a `SystemButton.Check` toggle preset (green while checked).
