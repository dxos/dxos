`@dxos/app-toolkit/ResetDialog` is the fatal error dialog an app shows in place of itself: the error, its stack, a copy of both and Reload, with optional handlers that each add a button (reset, safe mode, recovery, log download, a crash report). `SceneEditor.Root` takes an `overlay` that its viewport draws in every role, so a section or card shows what an article does, such as collaborators' cursors.
