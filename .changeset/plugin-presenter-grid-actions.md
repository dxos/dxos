In the presentation grid, double-clicking a slide or adding one opens it in the slide editor. The add button is a split button whose caret sets the layout of later slides, and a ghost tile after the last slide adds one. A slide with `hide: true` in its frontmatter is skipped when presenting and shown dimmed in the grid. The toolbar presents with the editor's present icon, replacing the fullscreen button. The slide editor splits evenly until resized and has no fold gutter. A canvas drawing on a slide is read-only and shows no toolbar, palette or panels. `Canvas.Root` takes `transparent`, which draws no surface under the scene; a canvas on a slide uses it. In fullscreen, the space around a letterboxed slide takes the slide's background. The canvas background's context menu has a Back item inside a frame or an isolated composite. The app has one context menu: right-clicking an element marked with `AppNode.contextMenuProps(id)` opens that graph node's actions, as its navtree menu lists them; navtree rows and presentation grid tiles are marked, and hiding a slide is a graph action. A presentation's properties lay out its theme, colors and fonts in pairs, show the theme's default fonts when unset, and render an example slide in the story; each color is a text box with a picker at its end. `@dxos/react-ui` adds `ColorPicker` (Ark's color picker: a wheel tab and a swatch grid of the theme's hues by tone, with a reset), and `@dxos/react-ui-form` adds `ColorField` and fixes `Form.Layout` dropping the title of an annotated optional field. Presenter is tagged beta and on by default in Composer. The DXOS Architecture space template includes an ECHO presentation. `decorateMarkdown` takes `nodes`, to decorate only some syntax (the slide editor decorates fenced code); the fullscreen pager ends with an exit button and drops the `[ESC]` hint. Breaking: a presentation's slides are `Slide` objects (`Presentation@0.2.0`; a migration converts 0.1.0 presentations in place): each refs its content and holds its title, hidden flag, notes and a generated image, and a presentation gains a description. Opening a slide opens its `Slide`, so the assistant beside it has the slide as context, and the presenter skill's `GenerateSlideImage` tool generates an image through plugin-studio, stores it as a file through plugin-file (`FileOperation.CreateFromSource`), and places it in the slide's layout; plugin-studio exports `StudioHooks`, and slide images accept `blob:` URLs. plugin-studio is now public.
