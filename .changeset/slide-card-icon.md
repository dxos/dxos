A slide `<Card>` takes an `icon`: a Phosphor glyph that stands with the card's title as one header, in the card's `tone`, over bullets sized for the card; the icon cards of a grid row start their bullets level. `<Grid align>` (`start`, `center`, `end`) gives a grid the height of what it holds in place of filling the slide.
