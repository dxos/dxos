Agents can get identities of their own and join spaces the way people do, behind plugin-agent's "Create identities for agents" setting, which is off by default; off, agents are created as before, with a brain named by their id. With it on, creating an agent waits about a second for EDGE to mint its identity and a space it owns, and the agent's brain is named by that identity's DID (`AgentPresence.brainId`); an agent EDGE cannot provision opens without an identity and works as agents always have. The agent space's members who can edit it are the agent's admins, Profile › Agents lists the agents you administer, and only their admins add an agent to other spaces, from a space's "Add from contacts" picker (`AppCapabilities.InviteeSource`, `AgentSpace.administeredAgents`), where it keeps one brain and one conversation with you. A watch is delivered in the space it was set from (`Trigger.origin`, `AgentPresence.delivers`). An agent's identity is admitted as an editor of each shared space it is in; a locked (personal or private) space admits no one after its genesis, an agent included. Account panels carry explicit positions (`Account.position`).
