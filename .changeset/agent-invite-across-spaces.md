An agent's admins can add it to their other spaces from a space's Members › "Add from contacts" picker (`AgentOperation.InviteAgent`) while plugin-agent's "Create identities for agents" setting is on. The agent keeps one brain across the spaces it is in, and each member keeps one conversation with it, which opening the agent in any of those spaces continues.
