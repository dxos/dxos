Agents can join other spaces the way people do: a space's Members panel offers your agents from other spaces in its "Add from contacts" picker (through the new `AppCapabilities.InviteeSource`). An agent added this way keeps one brain across its spaces, so its Recall tool finds what it learned anywhere, and opening it from another space continues your one conversation with it. A space with no agent can create one from "Add to space", and the create dialog offers one entry per type.
