Agents get identities of their own and join spaces the way people do. Creating an agent waits about a second for EDGE to mint its identity and a space it owns, and the agent's brain is named by that identity's DID (`AgentPresence.brainId`); an agent EDGE cannot provision opens without an identity and works as agents always have. The agent space's members who can edit it are the agent's admins, Profile › Agents lists the agents you administer, and only their admins add an agent to other spaces, from a space's "Add from contacts" picker (`AppCapabilities.InviteeSource`, `AgentSpace.administeredAgents`), where it keeps one brain and one conversation with you. A watch is delivered in the space it was set from (`Trigger.origin`, `AgentPresence.delivers`). An agent's identity is admitted as a member of each space it is in, marked as an agent (`SpaceMember.agent`, `Space.admitContact(contact, role, { agent: true })`), which the owner of a locked space may still do though the space takes no other members. `Space.admitContact` now rejects an admission the space would drop, rather than writing it and resolving, and a space drops a membership credential that names no subject. Account panels carry explicit positions (`Account.position`), and a space with no agent can create one from "Add to space".
