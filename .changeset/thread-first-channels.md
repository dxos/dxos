Channels are now thread-first. The main view lists root messages only, each showing the thread branching from it (name, reply count, last activity) or an affordance to start one. Creating a thread is deliberate — it marks its root message with an `org.dxos.chat.thread` annotation, so a message nobody threaded is not a thread — and every thread appears as a child of its channel in the navtree, opening as its own plank beside the channel where the composer posts back into that thread and quote-reply is offered. A thread is named from its navtree row, by any participant, and the name lives on that same annotation. Messages gain emoji reactions — folded per emoji with counts and an own-state accent, and stored as per-author feed items so an offline retry cannot double-count. Message hover controls are now a menu-driven toolbar: three quick reactions sit inline with a button opening the full emoji picker beside them, and edit and delete are in an overflow menu; editing is an explicit mode where Enter saves, Shift+Enter breaks the line and Escape discards. Deleting is restricted to your own messages, where any participant could previously delete anyone's. Message lists seat at the foot of their viewport and stay pinned to the newest message while the reader is at the bottom, and the hover controls float over the message rather than reserving a column beside it.
