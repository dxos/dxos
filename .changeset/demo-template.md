New Demo space template: sixteen chess games at every stage, a world map of every Cloudflare data centre with a globe tour from Los Angeles to Sydney, a voxel creeper and a voxel town. A game now has an optional `description`, which chess keeps set to the current FEN so tables and cards show each position; chess cards draw their board full width again, and the move list drops its focus ring. A map's `coordinates` are its globe tour route; the globe gets a light street-map palette, small dark-blue markers, a red tour route and a margin at rest, and flat-map pins shrink to 60%. The DXOS Architecture template's metrics sheet gains a pie of the busiest plugins' PR share.
