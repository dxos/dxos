`TaskList.Root` takes a `mnemonicVariant` prop (and `TaskMnemonic` a `variant`) choosing how a row
draws its mnemonic: the hued `tag` chip (the default), or `text`, `hue`, `outline`, `dot` or
`trailing`, which drop the chip's fill so the mnemonic stops reading as one more tag.
