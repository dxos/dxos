`diffView` takes `orientation: 'changes'` to read the editor's document as the newer version, with a line-first diff that stays precise on long documents, and `collapseUnchanged` to fold unchanged runs. Smart links on a lattice route about six times faster with the same result, and dragging a node no longer re-runs the graph's arrangement.
