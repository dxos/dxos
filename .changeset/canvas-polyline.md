Add a polyline shape and drawing tool (Y): each click places a vertex, Enter or a double-click ends the line open, and clicking the first vertex closes it into a filled shape; `params.closed` toggles it afterwards. A shape type can draw its frame without a fill (`NodeDef.filled`) and create itself from drawn points (`NodeDef.createFromPoints`), and the tools panel lists the polygon before the cloud. A shape's Rounded switch now has two distinct states: off draws square corners and on the look's rounded radius, while unset keeps the look's own corner; rotation and ports per side share a row in the properties panel. Double-clicking a shape (or Enter with it selected alone) enters its edit mode, left by Enter, Escape or a press outside: a box edits its label as before, and a polyline shows its vertices to drag, add (a segment's midpoint) or remove (Backspace), each edit one undo step. A type picks its edit mode with `NodeDef.editor` (`'label'` or `'vertices'`), and `CanvasAtoms.editing` is now an `Editing` union (`kind: 'label' | 'vertices'`), replacing `EditingPart`. ECHO adds content migrations: `Annotation.ContentVersionAnnotation` marks the field holding an object's content version, `Migration.defineContent` declares one step of that format, `db.runMigrations` applies each object's chain of steps writing only the changed records, and `Migration.upgradeContent` applies the same chain in memory. The canvas is its first user: new canvases are written at `dxos.org/scene/2`, and one step upgrades `dxos.org/scene/1` canvases, folding in today's read-time upgrades and the root scene rename; drawing variants now match a canvas by format at any revision. A canvas saved by a newer Canvas plugin (a later `dxos.org/scene/<n>`) opens read-only under a banner, and `CanvasOperation.Apply` refuses to edit it.
