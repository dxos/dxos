PLEX and TILE have icons of their own in `@dxos/brand`'s `assets/icons`, with black and white variants, and both marks are redrawn: PLEX has four of its six lines cut where they cross another, and TILE is its diamond cut into quarters three times over. The PLEX and TILE backgrounds draw the new marks. `CanvasEditor.Root` takes `fitZoom`, the highest zoom a fit reaches, so a host can open a small scene smaller than 1:1; the calendar's week view scrolls in a react-ui `ScrollArea`; and the zoom and action buttons on a globe or a map sit closer together.
