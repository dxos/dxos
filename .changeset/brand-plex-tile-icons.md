PLEX and TILE have icons of their own in `assets/icons`: `plex.svg` and `tile.svg`, with `black/icon-*.svg` and `white/icon-*.svg` variants, drawn at the size and line weight of the ECHO and KUBE icons. The PLEX mark is now woven: three of its six lines are cut where they cross another, so the lines read as passing over and under each other, and the PLEX backgrounds draw it with a ray running out from each end of each line. `plex-alt.svg` is a second cut of the PLEX mark, kept beside it for comparison.
