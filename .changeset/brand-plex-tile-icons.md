PLEX and TILE have icons of their own in `assets/icons`: `plex.svg` and `tile.svg`, with `black/icon-*.svg` and `white/icon-*.svg` variants, drawn at the size and line weight of the ECHO and KUBE icons. The PLEX mark is redrawn: four of its six lines are cut where they cross another, which leaves an arrowhead pointing left inside the star, and the PLEX backgrounds draw it with a ray running out from each end of each line.
