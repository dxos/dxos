PLEX and TILE have icons of their own in `assets/icons`: `plex.svg` and `tile.svg`, with `black/icon-*.svg` and `white/icon-*.svg` variants, drawn at the size and line weight of the ECHO and KUBE icons. Both marks are redrawn. PLEX has four of its six lines cut where they cross another, which leaves an arrowhead pointing left inside the star. TILE keeps its diamond and is cut into quarters three times over, in place of the tangram. The PLEX and TILE backgrounds draw the new marks, with a ray running out from each line end on the mark's edge.
