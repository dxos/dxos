'@dxos/react-ui': minor
---

Popover, tooltip, menu and hover-card arrows show whole on every side: a popup is now its own stacking context, so its arrow paints over the popup's surface and ring instead of beneath them. A hover preview (a reference chip's card) stays open while the pointer is over it, so its menu can be reached: `Popover.Content` marks itself as popover content again. The shell's HALO dialogs (joining a space or identity, the identity and space panels, invitations, reset) are rebuilt on the react-ui Dialog's columns: each step is a `Dialog.Body` of content in the dialog's content column and a `Dialog.Footer` of actions on one line, so title, content and actions share one edge; the identity chooser is a keyboard-navigable `Listbox` of full-width, equal-height options. `Dialog.Body` takes a `gap` and an `align='center'`, and stands its content a gap clear of the Header and Footer; a dialog sizes to its active step rather than its tallest one. `PinInput` takes a `size`, and a `Container` takes an `xl` gap. Breaking: `@dxos/shell/react` no longer exports `ActionBar`, `Action`, `CloseButton`, `Heading`, `InputLabel`, `Label`, `TextInput` or `stepStyles`; use the react-ui Dialog, Field and Input primitives. A list row's focus ring paints over its parts (an overlay above the row, which is its own stacking context), so it encloses the whole row rather than hiding under a leading tile or trailing tag. The shell's device, member, invitation and contact lists share one `Listbox` row composition (leading icon or avatar, text, trailing controls) with arrow-key navigation; breaking: `DeviceList` takes `ShellDevice[]` (its heading and add button move to the identity panel), `IdentityListItem` drops `onClick` (the list picks via `onSelect`), and `InvitationListProps` drops `className`, and the unreachable `InvitationAccepted` step is removed. A space invitation card sits on its host card's columns, with a smaller avatar. `Syntax.Content` is unpadded, so the panel hosting it owns the frame. Canvas shortcut labels show a shifted digit as its key cap (`⇧0`, not `⇧)`).
