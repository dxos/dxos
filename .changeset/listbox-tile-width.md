`Listbox.Root` takes `tileWidth`: items lay out as tiles at least that many pixels wide, as many columns as fit, reflowing as the container resizes instead of stretching to a fixed column count; the arrow keys move by the columns on screen. The presenter's slide grid uses it, with a small/large thumbnail toggle and captions from each slide's title or first heading.
