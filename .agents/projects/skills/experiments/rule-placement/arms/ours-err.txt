- **Never suppress unhandled errors to go green.** Do not set `dangerouslyIgnoreUnhandledErrors` in any vitest config, and do not swallow unhandled rejections — surface them and fix the root cause (a suppressed teardown race hides real failures). Tolerate a specific known signature only via a narrowly-scoped `onUnhandledError`, never a blanket ignore. Full rule → `code-style` skill.
