# ECHO presentation: speaking track (professional)

An alternative to [SPEAKING-TRACK.md](SPEAKING-TRACK.md) in a measured, professional register: the same story and
slides, with no jokes. Every slide fits its current hold in `record.mjs`, so either track plays against the same video.
Lines are paced at about 150 words a minute (2.5 words a second), starting half a second after the slide is entered. A
line marked _(verify)_ makes a product claim the code does not yet support as spoken.

## 2. In the Beginning (`#beginning`)

- SaaS transformed how companies buy software.

## 3. Your data lives inside SaaS (`#silo`)

- Companies stopped running their own servers.

## 4. Data in both directions (`#silo-comms`)

- Updates arrived continuously, onboarding took minutes, and costs fell.

## 5. The vendor grows (`#scale`)

- Vendors gained scale, serving thousands of customers on one platform.

## 6. Your competitors (`#competitors`)

- Scale also changed the economics.
- Switching became easier in principle, so vendors invested in lock-in.

## 7. Many silos (`#spread`)

- Most companies now run dozens of SaaS products.

## 8. Teams and their apps (`#spread-comms`)

- Each team chooses its own, and each holds a copy of company data.

## 9. A frontier lab arrives (`#frontier`)

- Then came generative AI.

## 10. Everyone talks to the lab (`#frontier-comms`)

- Employees adopted agents, and gave them broad access to company data.
- Every SaaS vendor added its own AI, integrated with the frontier platforms.

## 11. The lab at the centre (`#orbit`)

- The frontier labs, meanwhile, ship more capable models.
- And tooling that replaces many standalone applications.

## 12. The apps are absorbed (`#absorb`)

- Applications they can replace are absorbed into their platforms.
- Together with the data they hold.

## 13. Three labs (`#rivals`)

- As models converge, each lab competes on its platform.

## 14. The labs merge (`#merge-1`)

- This has already driven partnerships and acquisitions.
- Over time, it may consolidate the industry.

## 15. The Final Frontier (`#final-frontier`)

## 16. The Singularity (`#engulf`)

- Concentrating data, compute and intelligence in very few hands.

## 17. Don't Panic (`#dont-panic`)

- It doesn't have to be this way.

## 18. Back to your company (`#spread-again`)

- Instead of outsourcing your data and compute,

## 19. Your data, in ECHO (`#echo-hub`)

- DXOS lets companies build their own agent-driven applications, and retain custody of their data. _(verify: replicas sync through DXOS EDGE)_

## 20. The ECHO hypergraph (`#echo-spaces`)

- The ECHO hypergraph lets teams create private spaces.
- Each space connects to internal and external data sources.

## 21. A graph of objects (`#objects`)

- A space holds documents, files, records and messages, as linked objects.

## 22. One object (`#object-detail`)

- Composer provides over a hundred plugins: interoperable components, tools and agent skills.

## 23. People and agents together (`#object-thread`)

- People and agents collaborate in real time, in a shared context.
- The artifacts they produce sync back into the space.

## 24. Work that builds on itself (`#object-created`)

- They are available to everyone with access to it.
- Agents run configurable workflows across teams.

## 25. Everyone at work (`#echo-spaces-again`)

- DXOS becomes the operating system for the enterprise.
- ECHO becomes its knowledge base: flexible, secure, and under your control.

## 26. Your company again (`#echo-hub-again`)

- Compute and intelligence, brought in house. _(verify: functions run on Cloudflare Workers)_

## 27. The silos fold in (`#saas-merge`)

- And over time, less dependence on external vendors.

## 28. DXOS (`#company-fills`)

- This is DXOS.
- Your team.
- Your apps.
- Your data.
