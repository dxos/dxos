# ECHO presentation: speaking track (in the manner of Stephen Fry)

An alternative to [SPEAKING-TRACK.md](SPEAKING-TRACK.md), written to be read in the manner of Stephen Fry: urbane,
warm and wry, with British spelling and the odd aside. Slide 17's "Don't panic" is the natural cue for that voice. Every
slide fits its current hold in `record.mjs`, so the track plays against the same video. Lines are paced at about 150
words a minute (2.5 words a second), starting half a second after the slide is entered; this voice reads well a touch
slower, so leave the beats in. A line marked _(verify)_ makes a product claim the code does not yet support as spoken.

## 1. Blank (`#blank`)

- _(Silence; press → to begin.)_

## 2. In the Beginning (`#beginning`)

- In the beginning, there was SaaS.

## 3. Your data lives inside SaaS (`#silo`)

- It was, frankly, rather marvellous.

## 4. Data in both directions (`#silo-comms`)

- No servers humming in the basement.
- Software simply arrived, like the morning milk.

## 5. The vendor grows (`#scale`)

- Vendors discovered the delightful arithmetic of scale.
- One platform, thousands of customers.

## 6. Your competitors (`#competitors`)

- Scale, like most things, has its pros and cons.
- Leaving was easier. In theory. But, every vendor dug a deep moat.

## 7. Many silos (`#spread`)

- Soon every company was juggling dozens of them.

## 8. Teams and their apps (`#spread-comms`)

- Each team had its favourites, each with a copy of your data.

## 9. A frontier lab arrives (`#frontier`)

- And then, rather suddenly, came AI.

## 10. Everyone talks to the lab (`#frontier-comms`)

- We took to agents with enthusiasm, and handed them the keys to the house.
- Every vendor, naturally, bolted on an AI of its own.

## 11. The lab at the centre (`#orbit`)

- Meanwhile the frontier labs grew cleverer by the month.
- And their tools made many apps look rather superfluous.

## 12. The apps are absorbed (`#absorb`)

- One by one, the apps were gently absorbed.
- Their data, of course, went with them.

## 13. Three labs (`#rivals`)

- As the models grew alike, each lab sought to stand apart.

## 14. The labs merge (`#merge-1`)

- Partnerships followed, then acquisitions.
- Consolidation is rather the natural end of such stories.

## 15. The Final Frontier (`#final-frontier`)

- _(Silence; let the caption read.)_

## 16. The Singularity (`#engulf`)

- Until one enormous entity holds everything, and everyone.
- _(Beat, as the frame goes black.)_ Which is all perfectly fine. Up to a point.

## 17. Don't Panic (`#dont-panic`)

- Don't panic.
- It really needn't be this way.

## 18. Back to your company (`#spread-again`)

- Suppose, instead, you kept your data at home.

## 19. Your data, in ECHO (`#echo-hub`)

- DXOS lets you build your own agent-driven applications, without giving your data away. _(verify: replicas sync through DXOS EDGE)_

## 20. The ECHO hypergraph (`#echo-spaces`)

- ECHO, a hypergraph — if you'll forgive the word _(light chuckle)_ — gives teams private spaces.
- Each draws on the sources it needs.

## 21. A graph of objects (`#objects`)

- Inside, a constellation of documents, files, records and messages.

## 22. One object (`#object-detail`)

- Composer brings over a hundred plugins to the party: components, tools and agent skills.

## 23. People and agents together (`#object-thread`)

- People and agents work side by side, in real time, sharing the same context.
- What they make finds its way back into the space.

## 24. Work that builds on itself (`#object-created`)

- Where everyone else can admire it, or improve it.
- And agents, tireless creatures, keep the workflows moving between teams.

## 25. Everyone at work (`#echo-spaces-again`)

- DXOS becomes, in effect, the operating system of the enterprise.
- And ECHO its memory: flexible, secure, and entirely your own.

## 26. Your company again (`#echo-hub-again`)

- Your compute and intelligence, home where they belong. _(verify: functions run on Cloudflare Workers)_

## 27. The silos fold in (`#saas-merge`)

- And the vendors, one by one, become rather less essential.

## 28. DXOS (`#company-fills`)

- This is DXOS.
- Your team.
- Your apps.
- Your data.
