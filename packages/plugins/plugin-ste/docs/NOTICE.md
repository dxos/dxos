The specification ASD-STE100, Simplified Technical English, is the property of ASD (AeroSpace and Defence
Industries Association of Europe), Brussels, Belgium. ASD-STE100 is a registered trade mark of ASD.

of speech and their forms — not the approved meanings, definitions or examples. The full specification is free
