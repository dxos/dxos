# Welcome to Composer

Composer is a local-first workspace where your documents, data, and AI agents live in the same place.
Your data is stored on your device and syncs to the devices and with people you choose.

## Privacy First

You own your data. Keep it on-device only, or sync through DXOS EDGE services to collaborate with others and run agents — either way it stays yours, readable only by the people and agents you invite.

## Profile

Your identity, permissions, and keys are securely managed in your HALO — no account, no password. Click your avatar at the bottom left to open your Profile, where you can set your display name and connect other devices. Connect a second device early: your keys live on this one, so a synced device is also your backup.

## Spaces

Everything you make lives in a space — a collection of interconnected objects: documents, tables, sketches, contacts, and more. Spaces can be private or shared: press **Share** in the top bar to invite someone, and you edit together in real time, every change syncing as it happens.

## Plugins

Composer is a shell: every capability — data types, UI components, functions — comes from a plugin, and you control which are installed. Documents, sketches, tables, the inbox, the calendar, and the assistant are all plugins; you can build your own too. Plugins also contribute **companions** — tabs like chat or comments that open beside whatever you're working on.

## Getting started

Take the **Product Tour** from the Space Home page. The left sidebar is your navigation; the toolbar at the bottom left holds your Profile, Plugins, and Settings.
