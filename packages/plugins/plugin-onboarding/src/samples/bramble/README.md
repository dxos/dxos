# Space Tour

This is a **sample space**. It is the workspace of _Bramble Coffee Roasters_, a small specialty coffee company in Oakland. The data is fictional, so feel free to break, rename, or delete anything. Your own work lives in your **Personal** space (in the sidebar). When you're done exploring, you can delete this space entirely.

We made it so you can see what Composer is for without having to build a workspace from scratch.

## Take the tour

### Projects

Open **Projects** in the sidebar. Bramble runs four of them, each with its own tasks, documents and instructions for the agent:

- **Spring Blend Launch.** A new seasonal espresso blend going live in six weeks. Tasting protocol, cupping notes, the flavor wheel sketch, and tasks split between the roaster, the wholesale lead and operations.
- **Q2 Sourcing Trip.** Diego's two weeks visiting partner farms in Colombia and Ethiopia, with the itinerary and the lots he needs to lock.
- **Olive & Vine Onboarding.** Bringing a new wholesale account from samples to a first order, with a half-written welcome packet and the wholesale terms.
- **Roastery Ops.** Keeping the roasters running. It's blocked: a replacement part for the sample roaster is on backorder.

```prompt
What still needs to happen before we can ship the Spring Blend?
```

### Hand a task to the agent

Every project has a task or two with no one assigned and a description written as a brief: draft the product page, write a pre-trip brief for each farm, fill in the welcome packet, write a roaster maintenance checklist. Select one in the project's task list and choose **Assign selected tasks to agent**. The agent works in a session filed under the project and saves what it writes as a new artifact there.

Roastery Ops also owns a **Weekly roast report** routine. It's off. Turn it on and every Monday morning the agent reads the past week's roast log and writes a report into the project.

### Documents and sketches

Everything that belongs to a project lives in its **Artifacts**. A document can also sit in more than one place:

- The **Reference** collection holds company records that outlast any one project. **Wholesale terms** shows up in both the Olive & Vine and Spring Blend projects, and the **Esperanza cupping notes** in both Spring Blend and the sourcing trip. Delete either project and those documents stay put.
- The **Roastery Handbook** collection gathers what a new hire reads first: the floor plan and the tasting protocol. Both belong to their projects. The handbook only points at them.

The **About Bramble Coffee Roasters** document in the Welcome collection describes the company in detail: history, team, suppliers, customers, current work. It's a useful grounding doc if you want to ask the agent for new sample content that fits the same world.

## With the preview plugins

Some of Bramble's content uses plugins that are off by default. Turn them on from the Settings dialog and more of the space appears. The projects don't depend on any of it, but the agent reads the inbox when it's there.

### Inbox

About 35 emails grouped into conversations: supplier updates, wholesale reorders, internal memos, plus the newsletters and shipping notifications a real inbox has. The senders are the same people the projects mention. Expand the mailbox in the sidebar for **Starred**, **Important**, **Sent**, **All mail** and **Subscriptions**. A few conversations already carry a summary above the messages.

### Calendar

Meetings over the last two weeks and the next two: standups, customer cuppings, a site visit to a partner farm, a coffee expo.

### Contacts

The organizations Bramble works with, rendered three ways: a **Table**, a **Kanban** grouped by status, and a **Map** plotted from Oakland out to the partner farms in Colombia and Ethiopia. **People** lists the team and partner contacts, each linked to their organization.

### Spreadsheets and the roast log

The **Reference** collection also holds the green coffee inventory and the wholesale price list, both with live formulas. The **Roast Log** is a custom type defined by the space itself, shown as a table and a kanban by batch status.

## What Composer is

Composer is an extensible, local-first workspace. Documents, sketches, tasks, projects and agents, plus an inbox, a calendar, contacts and sheets when you turn them on. Everything lives in the same place, on your device, with no server in between. You can install plugins from the Settings dialog to add more content types.

**Private by default.** Your data lives on your device.

**Collaborative.** Invite someone to a space with the **Share** button in the top bar and you can edit together in real time.

**Open source.** [DXOS on GitHub](https://github.com/dxos/dxos).
