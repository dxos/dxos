The app is built with [DXOS](https://dxos.org/), a technology stack and SDK for local-first, collaborative apps.
[Composer](/docs/dxos/composer/) is built with it too. The app keeps diagrams the way Composer does, so the two apps
and the [DX CLI](/docs/dxos/cli/) read and write the same spaces.

## Identities and spaces

An **identity** is a key pair kept in the browser, with no account and no email address behind it. On your first
visit the app makes one for your browser.

A **space** is a small database that every member keeps a full copy of. Your diagrams live in spaces. Members are
identities: you, the people you shared the space with, and any Composer or CLI identity that joined it with a link.

## Peer to peer only, without a Composer account

The app has no sign-in, so its identity has no Composer account. For an identity like that, sync is peer to peer
only:

- Members' devices send changes to each other directly. DXOS's EDGE service helps them find each other, but it
  does not store or relay the space.
- Two members sync only while both are online. A change made while nobody else is online stays in your browser.
  It reaches the others the next time two of you are online together.
- The copy in your browser is the only copy that is yours. Clearing the site's data, or Reset in the app's error
  dialog, deletes it. Export a diagram as a `.dx.svg`, or share the space, to keep it somewhere else.

A Composer account adds EDGE sync. EDGE keeps a copy of your spaces and passes changes on to members who were
offline when you made them.

A drawing.sh member of a space that a Composer user shares still syncs peer to peer. It syncs with the Composer
user's devices, whenever one of them is online at the same time.
