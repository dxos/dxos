[Composer](https://dxos.org/composer/) is DXOS's collaborative local-first workspace. Its diagram editor is the
canvas the app uses. There a diagram is one more object in a space, next to documents, tasks and anything else your
team keeps.

Composer is in early access. [Request access](https://dxos.org/composer/) to use it.

## Spaces work in both apps

A space is the same thing in both apps, and so is the invitation link:

- **From the app to Composer.** Share the space in the app, then open the link in Composer, or paste it into
  Composer's join dialog. The space's diagrams show in Composer's navigator.
- **From Composer to the app.** Use Composer's share link for the space, and open it at
  [app.drawing.sh](https://app.drawing.sh/), or paste it into **Join with a link…** in the space menu. The app lists
  the space's diagrams. It does not show the space's other objects.

People in the same space see each other in either app. The avatars in the pill include Composer users, and
following someone works between the two apps.

A diagram made in the app is filed in the space's root collection, as Composer files one, so it shows in Composer
with the rest of the space.

## Files

A `.dx.svg` exported from Composer imports into the app with **Import .dx.svg…**, and one exported from the app
imports into Composer. [The .dx.svg file](/docs/files/) covers what the file holds.

## Agents in Composer

Composer's agents have a Drawing skill. Ask one for a diagram and it writes `.dx` statements, which the layout engine
places on the space's canvas. The result is an ordinary diagram in the space, so it opens in the app as well.

## Sync

With a Composer account, Composer syncs your spaces through EDGE, so changes reach your other devices and members
who were offline. The app has no account and syncs peer to peer only.
[drawing.sh and DXOS](/docs/dxos/#peer-to-peer-only-without-a-composer-account) explains what that means for a
space shared between the two.
