The app at [app.drawing.sh](https://app.drawing.sh/) is one full-screen canvas. Open it and draw. There is no
sign-up and nothing to install.

## Your first visit

On the first visit the app makes an [identity](/docs/dxos/#identities-and-spaces) for this browser without asking
anything. It then creates a [space](/docs/dxos/#identities-and-spaces) named **My diagrams** with one empty diagram
and opens it.

Everything is kept in the browser. A reload, or a later visit, reopens the space and diagram you had open. The
address bar names them (`/<space id>/<diagram id>`), so Back and Forward move between diagrams.

## The pill

The **pill** is the bar at the top center. From left to right it holds:

- **The space menu.** Search your spaces and switch between them. **New space** makes a space, and **Join with a
  link…** joins one from an invitation link. Each space's row menu renames or deletes it.
- **The diagram menu.** Search the space's diagrams, newest first. **New diagram** (<kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>N</kbd>) makes
  one, and **Import .dx.svg…** adds a file as a new diagram. Each diagram's row menu renames it, deletes it, or
  exports it as a `.dx.svg`.
- **Avatars** of everyone else in the space. Click one to jump to what they are looking at, or to follow them.
- **Share**, which makes an invitation link to the space.

The canvas's own toolbars float around it: shapes and connectors, undo and redo, zoom, and the inspector for the
selected shape.

## Working with others

**Share** makes an invitation link. Anyone who opens it joins the space and sees every diagram in it. While two
people are in the same space, each sees the other's cursor on the canvas. Edits arrive as they are made.

Members sync with each other directly, peer to peer. A change made while nobody else is online reaches the others
the next time two of you are online together.

## Composer

The app keeps its data the way [Composer](https://dxos.org/composer/) does. A space shared from the app opens in
Composer, and one shared from Composer opens here, with the same diagrams in both. [Composer](/docs/dxos/composer/)
covers this. [drawing.sh and DXOS](/docs/dxos/) covers how the app syncs without an account.

## Getting diagrams in and out

- **Export** in a diagram's row menu downloads it as `<name>.dx.svg`.
- **Import .dx.svg…** in the diagram menu adds a `.dx.svg` from the app, from Composer or from the CLI.
- A link can open a drawing in the app directly.

[The .dx.svg file](/docs/files/) covers each of these.
