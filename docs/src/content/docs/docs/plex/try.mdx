Composer is the app the PLEX pages build on. Trying it first shows what a plugin adds to: by the end of this
page you have a space with an object in it, open on two devices, and one more plugin turned on.

## Background

Composer keeps your data in spaces. A space is a set of objects that syncs as a unit, and sharing a space is how you
share its objects. You need no account to start: Composer creates an identity on the device the first time it opens.
[Spaces](/docs/echo/concepts/spaces/) explains what a space holds and how it syncs.

## Steps

1. **Open Composer.** Go to [composer.space](https://composer.space) in a browser, or install the desktop app from the
   same page. Composer opens on your personal space.

2. **Create a space and an object.** Click **+** beside **Spaces** in the navigation tree and name the space. Then click
   **+** on the new space and pick **Document**. Type a line into the document.

   The document appears under the space in the tree. It is saved on the device as you type; there is no save button.
   [Objects and relations](/docs/echo/concepts/objects-relations/) explains what the document is underneath.

3. **Share the space.** Open the space's menu and choose **Share**. Composer shows an invitation link. Open the link on a
   second device or in another browser profile, and enter the authorization code the first device shows.

   The space appears on the second device. Type in the document on either device, and the line shows up on the other
   one. [Invitations](/docs/halo/concepts/invitations/) explains the two codes.

4. **Turn on a plugin.** Open **Plugin Settings** in the rail and choose **Plugins**. Find **Kanban** and turn it on.
   Click **+** on your space again: **Kanban** is now in the list of things you can create.

## Recap

You can now:

- Create a space and an object in it.
- Share a space with another device or person, and watch an edit reach the other replica.
- Turn on a plugin from the plugin registry.

Every step here ran through plugins. To build one of your own, start with [Create a plugin](/docs/plex/learn/create-plugin/).
