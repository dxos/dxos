By the end of this page the contact book lists everyone you share a space with, once each, with the spaces you share.
You can also remove someone from a space.

## Background

A **contact** is anyone who shares a space with you. In most apps, a contact list is a table of its own, and the app
has to keep it up to date. The contact book keeps no list of its own. Instead, it reads each space's members and
merges them by DID, because a member's DID is the same in every space.

A **member** is an identity with an [access level](/docs/halo/concepts/spaces-membership/), which is `pull`, `read`,
`edit` or `admin`. Everyone in the contact book joined through an invitation or made the space, so everyone is an
admin, and an admin can remove anyone.

Removing a member is a **revocation**. It is a signed statement that undoes the delegation, the signed statement
that let the member in. The space's **group key**, which encrypts its changes, changes with it, so the removed member
cannot read anything written after. We remove a member in step 2.

To change a member's access level instead, see the [Space](/docs/halo/api/space/) API page.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **List your contacts.** The Contacts tab shows one row for each person. `useContacts` builds the rows. It follows
   `Space.spaces` and each ready space's `Space.members`, merges the members by DID, and leaves you out.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="contacts" lang="tsx" />

2. **Remove someone from a space.** The **×** beside a space in a contact's row calls `handleRemove`.
   `Space.removeMember` revokes that person's membership of that one space, and the space leaves their row.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="remove" lang="tsx" />

   `handleRemove` takes the person out of one space only. They stay in your contacts for as long as you share another.

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice, Bob and Carol are all in Book club, and Alice and Carol are in Climbing. Open Contacts on any device. Alice
sees Bob with Book club, and Carol with Book club and Climbing.

Remove Carol from Climbing on Alice's device, and Climbing leaves her row.
