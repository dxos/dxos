By the end of this page the contact book lists everyone you share a space with, once each, with the spaces you share,
and you can remove someone from one of them.

## Before you start

A contact is anyone who shares a space with you. The contact book keeps no list of its own: it reads each space's members
with `Space.members` and merges them by DID, the name every member has in every space.

A member is an identity with an access level: `pull`, `read`, `edit` or `admin`. Removing a member is a revocation, the
signed opposite of the delegation that let them in. The space's group key changes with it, so they cannot read anything
written after. An admin can remove anyone; changing a member's access instead is on the
[Space](/docs/halo/api/space/) API page.

Both pieces go in a new file, `contacts.ts`.

<Steps>

1. **List your contacts.** For each space in `spaces`, follow `Space.members`, then merge the lists by DID, leaving out
   yourself. `Stream.switchMap` starts over when you join or leave a space, and `Stream.zipLatestAll` emits whenever any
   space's members change. The Contacts tab follows this.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/contacts" region="contacts" />

2. **Remove someone from a space.** `Space.removeMember` revokes their membership. The **×** on a space beside a contact
   calls this; if it was the last space you shared, they leave your contacts too.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/contacts" region="remove" />

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice, Bob and Carol are all in Book club, and Alice and Carol are in Climbing. Open Contacts on any device: Alice sees
Bob in one space and Carol in two. Remove Carol from Climbing on Alice's device, and Climbing leaves her row. Remove Bob
from Book club, the only space Alice shares with him, and he leaves Alice's contacts.

## What you can do now

You can list everyone you share a space with and remove someone from a space. Next, [Add a device](/docs/halo/learn/add-device/)
puts the same contacts on your phone.
