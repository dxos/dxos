By the end of this page the contact book lists everyone you share a space with, once each, with the spaces you share,
and you can remove someone from a space.

## Background

A contact is anyone who shares a space with you. The contact book keeps no list of its own: it reads each space's members
and merges them by DID, since a member's DID is the same in every space.

A member is an identity with an access level: `pull`, `read`, `edit` or `admin`. Removing a member is a revocation, the
signed opposite of the delegation that let them in. The space's group key changes with it, so they cannot read anything
written after. An admin can remove anyone; changing a member's access instead is on the
[Space](/docs/halo/api/space/) API page.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **List your contacts.** `useContacts` returns everyone who shares a space with you, once each, with the spaces you
   share. It follows `Space.spaces` and each space's `Space.members`, leaves you out, and re-renders when someone joins,
   leaves or comes online. The Contacts tab shows a row for each contact.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="contacts" lang="tsx" />

2. **Remove someone from a space.** `Space.removeMember` revokes their membership. The **×** on a space beside a contact calls this, and
   that space leaves their row.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="remove" lang="tsx" />

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice, Bob and Carol are all in Book club, and Alice and Carol are in Climbing. Open Contacts on any device: Alice sees
Bob with Book club, and Carol with Book club and Climbing. Remove Carol from Climbing on Alice's device, and Climbing leaves
her row.
