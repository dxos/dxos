By the end of this page the contact book lists everyone you share a space with, once each, with the spaces you share.
You can also remove someone from a space.

## Background

A **contact** is anyone who shares a space with you. In most apps, a contact list is a table of its own, and the app
has to keep it up to date. The contact book keeps no list of its own. Instead, it reads each space's members and
merges them by DID, because a member's DID is the same in every space.

A **member** is an identity with an access level, which is `pull`, `read`, `edit` or `admin`.

Removing a member is a **revocation**, the signed opposite of the delegation that let them in. The space's group key
changes with it, so the removed member cannot read anything written after. We remove a member in step 2.

An admin can remove anyone. To change a member's access instead, see the [Space](/docs/halo/api/space/) API page.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **List your contacts.** `useContacts` returns everyone who shares a space with you, once each, with the spaces you
   share. It follows `Space.spaces` and each space's `Space.members`, leaves you out, and re-renders when someone joins,
   leaves or comes online. The Contacts tab shows a row for each contact.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="contacts" lang="tsx" />

2. **Remove someone from a space.** `Space.removeMember` revokes their membership. The **×** on a space beside a
   contact calls this, and that space leaves their row.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Contacts" region="remove" lang="tsx" />

   Notice that `handleRemove` passes the space's id and the contact's DID.

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice, Bob and Carol are all in Book club, and Alice and Carol are in Climbing. Open Contacts on any device. Alice
sees Bob with Book club, and Carol with Book club and Climbing.

Remove Carol from Climbing on Alice's device, and Climbing leaves her row.
