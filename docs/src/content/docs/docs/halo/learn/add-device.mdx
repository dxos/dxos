By the end of this page Alice's phone joins her identity, and shows her name, her spaces and her contacts.

## Background

Alice opens the contact book on her phone, and it asks for her name as if she were new. The phone is a device of its
own, with its own key, and a device that already has the identity has to let it in.

Adding a device is the same handshake as inviting a person, with a device invitation in place of a space invitation.
The **host** is the device already in the identity, which starts the invitation. The **guest** is the new device,
which redeems it. At the end, the host writes a delegation, a signed statement that makes the new device's key a
member of the identity's group.

The new device then syncs every space the identity is in, from the other device or through EDGE, DXOS's always-online
peer. We don't type in anything about the identity again.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Share your identity.** On the Devices tab, **Add a device** calls `handleAdd`. `Identity.share` starts a device
   invitation on the device we have, and `Invitation.code` returns its code.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="share" lang="tsx" />

   `Identity.share` takes the same options as `Space.share`, and `shared-secret` matters more here. Without it, anyone
   who saw the invitation code could add a device of their own to Alice's identity.

2. **Join from the new device.** The phone has no identity yet, so we start from its welcome screen. **Join your
   other device** opens the `Guest` panel from [Invite people](/docs/halo/learn/invite-people/), which calls
   `handleJoin` with the pasted code.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Welcome" region="join" lang="tsx" />

   `Identity.join` returns a flow, as `Space.join` does. The handshake and its authorization code are the same as for
   a space, so `Guest` needs no changes.

3. **List your devices.** The Devices tab reads its list from `useDevices`, which follows the `Identity.devices`
   stream. Each device carries its `key`, its `kind` and its `presence`. `current` is true for the device the code
   runs on.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="list" lang="tsx" />

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice's laptop has her identity, her spaces and her contacts. Her phone has nothing.

1. On the laptop, open **Devices**, press **Add a device** and copy the code.
2. On the phone, press **Join your other device**, paste it and press **Join**.
3. Type the authorization code the laptop shows into the phone and press **Send**.

The phone now shows Alice's name, both spaces and both contacts, and each device lists the other.
