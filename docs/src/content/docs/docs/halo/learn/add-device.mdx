By the end of this page Alice's phone joins her identity, and shows her name, her spaces and her contacts.

## Background

Each device has its own key. Adding a device is the same handshake as inviting a person, with a device invitation
instead of a space invitation: the device already in the identity is the host, and the new device the guest. At the end,
the host writes a delegation that makes the new device a member of the identity's group.

The new device then syncs the identity's personal space and every space the identity is in, from the other device or
through EDGE. Nothing about the identity has to be typed in again.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Share your identity.** `Identity.share` starts a device invitation on the device you have. **Add a device** on the
   Devices tab calls this and shows the code.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="share" lang="tsx" />

2. **Join from the new device.** On the new device, before it has an identity, `Identity.join` redeems the code. The
   handshake and its authorization code are the same as for a space, so the welcome screen hands the flow to the same
   guest panel as **Join**. **Join your other device** calls this.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Welcome" region="join" lang="tsx" />

3. **List your devices.** `useDevices` returns the identity's devices and re-renders when one joins or comes online. It
   follows `Identity.devices`. Each device has a key, a kind, whether it is online, and whether it is the device the code
   runs on. The Devices tab shows them.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="list" lang="tsx" />

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice's laptop has her identity, her spaces and her contacts; her phone has nothing.

1. On the laptop, open **Devices**, press **Add a device** and copy the code.
2. On the phone, press **Join your other device**, paste it and press **Join**.
3. Type the authorization code the laptop shows into the phone and press **Send**.

The phone now shows Alice's name, both spaces and both contacts, and each device lists the other.
