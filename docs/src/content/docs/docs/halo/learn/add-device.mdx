By the end of this page Alice's phone joins her identity, and shows her name, her spaces and her contacts.

## Background

With an account, a second device signs in with the same password. In HALO, each device has its own key, and a device
that already has the identity lets the new one in.

Adding a device is the same handshake as inviting a person, with a device invitation instead of a space invitation.
The device already in the identity is the host, and the new device is the guest. At the end, the host writes a
delegation that makes the new device a member of the identity's group.

The new device then syncs every space the identity is in, from the other device or
through EDGE. You don't have to type in anything about the identity again.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Share your identity.** `Identity.share` starts a device invitation on the device you have, and `Invitation.code`
   returns its code. **Add a device** on the Devices tab calls this and shows the code.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="share" lang="tsx" />

2. **Join from the new device.** On the new device, before it has an identity, `Identity.join` redeems the code. The
   handshake and its authorization code are the same as for a space, so the welcome screen hands the flow to the same
   guest panel as **Join**. **Join your other device** calls this.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Welcome" region="join" lang="tsx" />

3. **List your devices.** `useDevices` returns the identity's devices and re-renders when one joins or comes online.
   It follows `Identity.devices`.

   Each device carries its key, its kind, whether it is online, and whether it is the device the code runs on. The
   Devices tab shows them.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Devices" region="list" lang="tsx" />

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice's laptop has her identity, her spaces and her contacts. Her phone has nothing.

1. On the laptop, open **Devices**, press **Add a device** and copy the code.
2. On the phone, press **Join your other device**, paste it and press **Join**.
3. Type the authorization code the laptop shows into the phone and press **Send**.

The phone now shows Alice's name, both spaces and both contacts, and each device lists the other.
