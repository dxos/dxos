By the end of this page you can make named spaces, such as Book club and Climbing, and switch between them.

## Background

Bob and Carol need somewhere to be before we can invite them. A **space** is a group of identities that replicate the
same data. ECHO keeps a database in each one. However, the contact book uses spaces only for who is in them.

In Keyhive's terms a space is a group, and the identity that creates it is its first admin. An admin can invite
people, change what members may do and remove them. We create a space in step 1.

A space takes a moment to become ready after we create it, and `Space.spaces` lists it before then. We list only the
ready ones, in step 2.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Create a space.** **New space** asks for a name and calls `handleCreate`. `Space.create` returns the new space's
   id, name and state at once.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Spaces" region="create" lang="tsx" />

   `Space.waitReady` is the line to keep. The space is ready a moment after `Space.create` returns, and the dropdown
   lists only ready spaces, so `onSelect` has to wait for it.

2. **List your spaces.** The dropdown takes its options from `useSpaces`. The hook follows the `Space.spaces` stream
   and keeps the spaces that are ready.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/ContactBook" region="spaces" lang="tsx" />

   `name` is optional on a space, so `named` leaves out any space that was made without one.

</Steps>

## Try it

Alice has a name and no spaces. Press **New space**, name it, and make another. The dropdown switches between them.
