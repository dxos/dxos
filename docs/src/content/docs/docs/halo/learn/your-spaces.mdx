By the end of this page you can make named spaces, such as Book club and Climbing, and switch between them.

## Background

A space is a group of identities that replicate the same data. ECHO keeps a database in each one; the contact book uses
spaces only for who is in them. In Keyhive terms a space is a group, and the identity that creates it is its first admin.
Admins invite people, change what members may do and remove them.

`Space.create` makes a space, and `Space.spaces` lists every space your identity belongs to. Your identity also has a
personal space, which only your own devices join; `Space.spaces` leaves it out.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Create a space.** `Space.create` returns the new space's id, name and state at once. The space opens a moment later,
   so wait for `Space.waitReady` before you use it, then select it. **New space** calls this.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/Spaces" region="create" lang="tsx" />

2. **List your spaces.** `useSpaces` returns the spaces that are ready, and re-renders when one is created, joined or
   left. It follows `Space.spaces`, which also lists spaces that are not open. The dropdown shows the named ones, by name.

   <Snippet file="halo-tutorial/app/ContactBook" region="spaces" lang="tsx" />

</Steps>
