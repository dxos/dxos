The HALO learning path builds one app: a contact book. You give yourself a name, keep a few named spaces, and invite
people into them. Your contacts are the people you share a space with, so the app never keeps a list of its own. It reads
each space's members and merges them.

## What the app is made of

Everything the contact book shows comes from HALO:

- **Your identity.** A Keyhive group named by a DID, with your name on it. The device you start on is its first member.
- **Your spaces.** Each space is a group too, with you as its first admin. ECHO keeps data in a space, but the contact book
  uses spaces only for who is in them.
- **Invitations.** Each person you invite joins through a short handshake that ends in a signed delegation.
- **Your devices and a recovery code.** Every device you add is another member of your identity, and so is the key behind
  a recovery code.

[Identities and devices](/docs/halo/concepts/identities-devices/) describes the model in full.

## The finished app

Here it runs for Alice, Bob and Carol. All three are in Book club, and Alice and Carol are also in Climbing. Open Contacts
on Alice's device to see Bob and Carol with the spaces each shares with her. Make a new space, invite someone into it, or
switch a device offline. To change the code and run it yourself, fork it on StackBlitz.

Each page of the learning path builds one piece of it:

| Page | File | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Your name](/docs/halo/learn/your-name/) | `client.ts`, `profile.ts` | A client, an identity with your name, and renaming |
| [Your spaces](/docs/halo/learn/your-spaces/) | `spaces.ts` | Named spaces, and a list to switch between them |
| [Invite people](/docs/halo/learn/invite-people/) | `invitations.ts` | Invite and Join, with an authorization code |
| [Your contacts](/docs/halo/learn/your-contacts/) | `contacts.ts` | Everyone you share a space with, and removing them |
| [Add a device](/docs/halo/learn/add-device/) | `devices.ts` | Your phone, with the same name, spaces and contacts |
| [Recover an identity](/docs/halo/learn/recover-identity/) | `recovery.ts` | A recovery code, and your identity back on a new device |

The code is Effect. Each function is an `Effect` or a `Stream` that needs the HALO services, and the app runs it with
`Effect.runPromise`. The pages show the app's HALO code; the components that draw it are in the project.
