Overlays open above the page, in a portal. Each takes the size of its trigger unless its content names a `size`.

## Which to use

| Overlay     | Opens on              | Holds focus | Use for                                                  |
| ----------- | --------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Dialog`    | Its trigger, or `open` | Yes        | A task the user finishes or cancels before going on      |
| `Popover`   | Its trigger           | With `modal` | Short content tied to one control, such as a share link  |
| `Tooltip`   | Hover or focus        | No          | A short name or hint for a control                       |
| `HoverCard` | Hover                 | No          | A preview of what a link or name points to               |
| `Toast`     | `open`                | No          | A notice that something happened                         |

## `Dialog`

<Snippet file="tile-components" region="dialog" lang="tsx" />

<TilePreview id="dialog" />

Parts: `Root`, `Trigger`, `Content`, `Header`, `Title`, `Description`, `Body`, `Footer` and `CloseTrigger`.
`Dialog.Root` takes `open` and `onOpenChange` to open it from code. `Dialog.CloseTrigger` with no children is a close
button; with `asChild` it makes its child close the dialog. `Dialog.Content` takes `size`. `Body` scrolls when the
content is taller than the screen.

## `Popover`

<Snippet file="tile-components" region="popover" lang="tsx" />

<TilePreview id="popover" />

The same parts as `Dialog`, plus `Popover.Anchor` to position the popover against an element other than its trigger.
`Popover.Content` takes `arrow` to draw an arrow toward the trigger.

## `Tooltip`

<Snippet file="tile-components" region="tooltip" lang="tsx" />

<TilePreview id="tooltip" />

Parts: `Root`, `Trigger` and `Content`. `Tooltip.Trigger` also takes `content` and `side` as a shorthand, with no
`Content` part. `Tooltip.TextTooltip` shows a text's full value when it is cut off. An icon-only button shows its label
as a tooltip without any of these.

## `HoverCard`

Parts: `Root`, `Trigger` and `Content`.

## `Toast`

<Snippet file="tile-components" region="toast" lang="tsx" />

<TilePreview id="toast" />

`Toast.Provider` goes once near the app's root, and `Toast.Toaster` is where toasts appear. Each `Toast.Root` is open
while its `open` is true, and closes after its `duration` or when the user closes it, reporting through
`onOpenChange`. Inside it: `Toast.Header` (with an `icon`), `Toast.Title`, `Toast.Description`, `Toast.Footer`,
`Toast.ActionTrigger` for a button that acts, and `Toast.CloseTrigger`.
