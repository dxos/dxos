## Before you start

TILE has four overlays, all drawn above the page:

- A `Dialog` is modal: the page behind it is inert until it closes.
- A `Popover` opens beside its trigger and closes when you click elsewhere.
- A `Tooltip` shows a short hint on hover or focus.
- A `Toast` reports something that happened and goes away by itself.

Each overlay has a `Trigger` that opens it and a `Content` that holds it. `asChild` on a trigger makes your button the
trigger, rather than wrapping it in another button. See [Overlays](/docs/tile/api/overlays/).

## Try it

Choose a contact and press Edit. Save a change, and the toast appears. Press Delete, then Keep or Delete.

## What you can do now

You can open dialogs and popovers from buttons, add tooltips to any element, and show toasts. Next,
[Icons and translations](/docs/tile/learn/icons-and-translations/) moves the screen's strings into a resource.
